My Top 5: Faith Hall of Fame

  1. Abraham

    Known as the father of faith, Abraham trusted God’s promise even when he could not see the outcome. His obedience set the foundation for generations.

  2. Moses

    Moses demonstrated extraordinary faith by leading Israel out of Egypt, confronting Pharaoh, and relying on God through impossible circumstances.

  3. David

    David’s faith was bold and fearless. From defeating Goliath to writing psalms of worship, he showed deep trust in God throughout his life.

  4. Daniel

    Daniel remained faithful even under pressure, refusing to compromise his devotion. His courage is one of Scripture’s strongest examples of unwavering faith.

  5. Paul

    Paul’s transformation and dedication to spreading the gospel demonstrate powerful faith. His letters continue to shape Christian teaching today.
